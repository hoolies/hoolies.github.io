01 — Philosophy

Fundamentals first.
Everything else follows.

Tools change weekly. TCP, the kernel, and how systems fail under load — that knowledge compounds forever. I teach the way I engineer: from first principles, with patience, until it clicks.

Deep Linux roots

Three decades from shell scripts to container orchestration at the edge. I know where the pain lives — in init systems, cgroups, networking stacks, and the gaps between documentation and reality.

Fundamentals evangelist

I don't hand people runbooks and walk away — I teach the reasoning behind them. On Amazon's L&D team I design, review, and approve training org-wide; in production I mentor engineers through live incidents until they can debug BGP, containers, or a SCADA flap without me in the room. The goal is always the same: make the fundamentals stick so the next person is never starting from zero.

Technology afficionado

From BGP peering sessions to business analytics with AI — I connect layers 1–7, on-prem to cloud, and engineering teams to the outcomes they actually need. If it's new, interesting, or broken in a fascinating way, I'm already hands-on with it.
