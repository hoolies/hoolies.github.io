01 · Philosophy

Fundamentals first.
Everything else follows.

Tools change daily, especially now with AI, LLMs, and MCP. But the TCP/IP stack, Windows and Linux kernels, and the core reasons systems and networks fail? That knowledge compounds forever. I teach the way I engineer: from first principles, with patience, until it clicks.

Deep Linux roots

Over three decades spanning shell scripts, container orchestration at the edge, and ephemeral shells. I know exactly where the pain lives and the vast gaps between documentation, expectations and reality.

Fundamentals evangelist

I never just hand someone a runbook and walk away. I teach people to act with purpose by understanding the reasoning and impact behind every decision. I have trained countless individuals across diverse technologies, including Linux script automation, APIs, databases, SQL, and AI.

Technology aficionado

From VPN sessions, BGP peering, and proxychain sockets, to Linux fleets, container configuration, databases, and AI business analytics. I connect layers 1 through 7, on-premise to cloud, and engineering teams to the outcomes they actually need.
