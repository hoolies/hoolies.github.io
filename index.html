Philosophy

Fundamentals first.
Everything else follows.

Tools change daily, especially now with AI, LLMs, and MCP. But the TCP/IP stack, Windows and Linux kernels, and the reasons systems and networks fail? That knowledge compounds forever. I demystify complex, high-risk infrastructure flaws, breaking them down into clear, actionable steps that eliminate outages and lower long-term maintenance costs.

Deep Linux roots

Over three decades spanning shell scripts, container orchestration at the edge, and ephemeral shells. I know exactly where the pain lives and the vast gaps between documentation, expectations and reality.

Fundamentals evangelist

I never just hand someone a runbook and walk away. I teach people to act with purpose by understanding the reasoning and impact behind every decision. I have trained engineers across Linux, automation, APIs, databases, SQL, and AI.

Technology aficionado

From VPN sessions, BGP peering, and proxychains with socket-level debugging, to SCADA and OT network integration, firewall and ACL design, Linux fleets, and AI business analytics. I connect layers 1 through 7, on-premise to cloud, and engineering teams to the outcomes they actually need.
